Method and limitations

Depth definition

Depth = hydrated reference thickness − hydrated residual thickness

The output is the macroscopic height difference after hydration, not ablation depth in the compressed state. Drying, shrinkage and repeated-use models are not included.

Pooling

The main and supplementary “3 mm” sheets are treated as repeated measurements of the same material, as confirmed by the project investigator. Valid per-record depths at identical settings receive equal weight. The mean uses all n records; sample SD uses n−1.

This is not an equal-weight average of the two sheet means. Pooling does not establish that all variation is measurement error or remove possible systematic differences.

Interpolation

Linear or bilinear interpolation uses adjacent pooled means only when every required endpoint has a numerical value. Missing, qualitative-only and cut-through endpoints are not bridged. No extrapolation, monotonic constraint or global fit is applied.

This empirical interpolator has not been independently validated as a general predictive model. Two decimal places do not imply 0.01 mm fabrication accuracy.

Record handling

  • Only hydrated measurements from the two power–speed sheets are used.
  • A same-row hydrated reference is required; missing baselines are not filled, including affected supplementary records at 30% and 40% power.
  • Bubbles, blanks and malformed values such as “17..2” are excluded. “No obvious groove” remains qualitative.
  • Any cut-through record at a required endpoint blocks a numerical estimate.
  • Main-sheet column F has an ambiguous speed header and is excluded by default. Interpreting it as 2000 mm/s is an explicit assumption; the supplementary header is unambiguous.
  • CSV export retains raw values, source labels, initial thicknesses and exclusions. Numerical records are not removed solely for deviating from the mean.

Scope

Results apply to the tested material and processing conditions, not arbitrary thicknesses or machines. Recalibrate after configuration changes and verify settings with a small test sample. Maintain laser enclosure, extraction and supervision.
